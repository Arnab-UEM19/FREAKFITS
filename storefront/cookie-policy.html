01 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device to remember information between visits or requests. We also use a couple of related browser-storage technologies — localStorage and sessionStorage — which work similarly but are only ever readable by our own site, not sent with every request the way a cookie is. Where this policy says "cookies," it covers both, unless we say otherwise.

03 Third-party cookies

When you check out, our payment partner Razorpay may set its own cookies within its hosted payment interface to process your transaction securely and prevent fraud. These are governed by Razorpay's own privacy and cookie practices, not ours. We don't control third-party cookies and recommend checking the relevant provider's policy if you want more detail.

04 Managing your preferences

Essential cookies can't be switched off, since the store (login, cart, checkout security) won't function without them. Where a cookie or storage item is optional, you can control it from our dedicated preferences page.

05 Controlling cookies via your browser

Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. Because essential cookies are required for the site to function, blocking them may prevent you from logging in, checking out, or using the cart. Clearing your browser's local storage will also empty a guest cart that isn't tied to a logged-in account.

06 Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the cookies and storage we use change — for example, if we introduce analytics or marketing tools in the future. We'll update the "Last updated" date whenever we do.
