Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to wear your colours with complete confidence. Please review our official return guidelines and initiate your claim below.

📹

Mandatory Unboxing Video

An uncut, continuous video of opening the sealed package upon delivery is strictly mandatory. Without an unboxing video, no return or refund claim will be entertained.

⏱️

48-Hour Return Window

Return and exchange requests must be initiated within 48 hours of parcel delivery. Requests made after 48 hours cannot be accepted by our logistics network.

🛡️

Factory Defects Covered

Only products with verified manufacturing or company-side defects (e.g. stitching tears, crest misprints) are eligible for return. Damage caused on the client's side will not be entertained.

🔄

Size Discrepancy & Refund

Need a different size? Pay the price of the new replacement size jersey. Once your returned kit is dispatched, the previous size amount will be refunded within 48 hours.
