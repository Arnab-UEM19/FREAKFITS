📐 How to Measure Your Body

1

Chest / Bust

Place a flexible tape measure around the fullest part of your chest, directly under your armpits. Keep the tape straight and snug without pulling too tight.

2

Jersey Length

Measure from the highest point of your shoulder seam straight down to where you want the bottom hem to rest (usually midway down your zipper/hips).

3

Shoulder Width

Measure across the upper back from the tip of the left shoulder bone horizontally across to the tip of the right shoulder bone.

⚡ Pro Match Fit vs Fan Cut

STADIUM / FAN FIT (Standard)

Designed for everyday comfort, terrace support, and gym training. Standard relaxed athletic cut that fits true to regular t-shirt sizing in India.

PRO MATCHDAY CUT (Slim)

Streamlined, aerodynamic cut designed for intense match play. Fits tighter around chest and shoulders. If between sizes or prefer looser room, choose 1 size up.
